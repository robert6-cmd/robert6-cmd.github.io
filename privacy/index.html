GRAVITY Balls Privacy Policy

Gravity Games · Effective date: November 21, 2026

In short:

  • GRAVITY Balls has no accounts of its own. It does not ask for your name, email address, photos, contacts, location, camera or microphone.
  • Players aged 13 and over can keep their progress in their Google account through Google Play Games (cloud saves). Players under 13 are never signed in.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device, and with Google Play Games if you use it. Gravity Games does not receive it.
  • When the game shows ads, they come from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked child-directed, ads are not personalised, and they are limited to family-friendly (G-rated) content.
  • The game contains no analytics, no tracking and no chat.

1. Who we are

GRAVITY Balls ("the game") is made by Gravity Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles and why. If you have any question, contact us at gravitygames.support@gmail.com.

2. Who the game is for

GRAVITY Balls is a game for everyone, and children may play it. Because some players may be children, the game asks for the year you were born the first time it starts. From that answer it saves only whether you are under 13 or not, never the year itself, and only on your device. We have designed the game so that it does not collect personal information from anyone, whatever their age, and ads are handled the same way for every player (see section 5).

3. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following in its private storage on your device, so you can continue where you left off:

WhatWhy
Game progress: scores, statistics, Gravity Cores, unlocked and selected cosmeticsTo keep your progress between sessions
Settings: music and sound volume, vibrationTo remember your preferences
Age group (under 13, or 13 and over), from the first-launch question. The birth year is not storedSo the game can treat younger players appropriately
Caches (generated music, graphics)To make the game load faster

This information never leaves your device except in the ways described below and in section 4. It contains no names or contact details.

  • Android backup. If backup is turned on in your device's settings, Android may copy the game's save file to your own Google account, so that your progress comes back when you reinstall the game or move to a new phone. This backup is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access it. Caches and other files are excluded.
  • Backup codes. In Settings → Progress Backup you can copy your progress as a text code and paste it back later. The code goes only to your device's clipboard; the game never sends it anywhere. Whether you store or share it is up to you.

4. Cloud saves with Google Play Games (players 13 and over)

If you answered the first-launch question as 13 or older, the game uses Google Play Games Services, a service of Google LLC, so your progress is kept in your Google account and comes back when you reinstall the game or play on another phone.

  • Sign-in. Google Play Games signs you in automatically with the Play Games profile on your device. If it does not, you can sign in from Settings → Progress Backup, or simply not use cloud saves. The game works the same either way.
  • What is saved. The same game progress and settings that are stored on your device (section 3), plus a short description (number of games and best score). It contains no names or contact details.
  • What Google handles. Google Play Games receives your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar, which the game never shows or stores) and the saved game, and Google's software collects diagnostic and usage data to keep the service working, under Google's Privacy Policy. Data is encrypted in transit. The game can only read and write its own saved game for your profile.
  • We do not receive it. Gravity Games has no server and cannot see your saved game or your Google account.
  • Players under 13 (or anyone who has not answered the age question) are never signed in to Google Play Games, and their progress stays on their device.

5. Advertising

The free version of the game may show ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show an ad, and to prevent fraud, Google's ads software in the game sends Google some information about the device and the app. This includes the IP address (which indicates an approximate location), the device type and operating system, how the app and the ad performed, and diagnostic data. It may also include an app-specific identifier used for fraud prevention and to limit how often the same ad appears.

We have configured the ads to collect as little as possible:

  • Every ad request is marked as child-directed, for all players. Under Google's rules, this means ads are not personalised, and are not based on your interests or on your activity in other apps or websites. There is no remarketing.
  • Ads are limited to content rated G (suitable for general audiences).
  • The game does not request the Android permission that gives access to the device's advertising ID.
  • We use Google AdMob only: no other ad networks and no ad mediation.
  • If you buy the optional "Remove Ads" upgrade (when it is offered), the ads software is not started at all.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not receive any information that identifies you from Google.

6. Purchases

The game may offer optional in-app purchases (for example, removing ads). Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. Google tells us only that a purchase was made, so the game can unlock it. Gravity Cores, the in-game currency, cannot be bought with real money.

7. What we do not do

  • We do not ask for, collect or store names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking services.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.
  • The game has no chat, no profiles of its own and no way to share content with other players.

8. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The game works the same way for every player, and every ad request is treated as coming from a child (section 5). Players under 13 are never signed in to Google Play Games (section 4). If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about your child's use of the game, contact us at gravitygames.support@gmail.com and we will help.

9. Keeping and deleting your information

We do not hold any of your information on our own systems, so there is nothing for us to delete. You are in control:

  • Game data on your device: uninstall the game, or clear its storage in Android Settings → Apps → GRAVITY Balls → Storage.
  • Android backup: manage or delete it in your Google account's backup settings.
  • Google Play Games cloud save: delete the game's data, or your whole Play Games profile, from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.
  • Ad-related data held by Google: see Google's Privacy Policy for your choices and rights.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. Because we do not collect or store personal information ourselves, we usually have nothing to provide. If you contact us, we will answer your question and point you to the right place for data held by Google.

11. Security

Game data stays in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read. Information sent to Google by the ads software and by Google Play Games is encrypted in transit.

12. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game's update notes.
